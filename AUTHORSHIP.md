# Authorship and adaptation

## Kaustubh Lall's work

Kaustubh Lall created and maintains this harness and its working system. His work
includes the cross-project preferences, task-dependent working methods, skill
routing, orchestration and integration design, ownership and authorization
boundaries, evidence standards, recovery practices, and their application across
coding-agent toolchains. The portable distribution, public export policy and
installation workflow are maintained as part of that system.

The direction of adaptation matters: selected techniques from ECC and other
upstream projects were adapted into Kaustubh's existing harness and workflows.
Upstream method credit does not transfer authorship of the surrounding harness,
its requirements or its integration design to those projects.

Original harness material and locally authored components are credited to
Kaustubh Lall. The root MIT license applies to the original material identified
in THIRD_PARTY.md. Changes to this bundle should retain that credit.

## Upstream work

Imported skills, reference collections, scripts and methods retain the credit
and licenses of their respective authors. This repository does not claim that
Kaustubh authored unchanged upstream material, including the ECC reference
corpus. Source manifests and adjacent notices identify those origins.

The bundle contains four kinds of material:

| Kind | Authorship and attribution |
|---|---|
| Original harness, preferences and integration | Kaustubh Lall |
| Locally adapted methods and entrypoints | Kaustubh Lall's adaptation and integration, with upstream method/content credit retained |
| Vendored upstream skills and references | Their original authors and licenses |
| Native tool-backed skills | Their providers; documented as restore dependencies, not redistributed here |

ECC-derived techniques and source are credited to Affaan Mustafa and the ECC
project. Other upstream credits include OpenAI, Notion Labs and the authors named
in each skill's source and license files. See [THIRD_PARTY.md](THIRD_PARTY.md).
